Setup

Backroad needs a free GraphHopper routing key. Sign up at graphhopper.com, open the dashboard and copy your API key in. It stays on this phone.

Tap anywhere on the map to start the loop somewhere other than your current position. Road measurements are reused while you stay in one area, so only the first search there is slow.

The free routing plan allows 500 credits a day. A loop is routed in legs of five stops, so most candidates cost two credits — roughly 40 searches a day at the default settings.
